Traditional Ecuadorian Food

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Traditional Ecuadorian Food

I chose this name because my website will be about the traditional dishes of Ecuador. I want people to learn more about the food we have in my country and why it is important to our culture.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to share information about traditional Ecuadorian food. Visitors will learn about popular dishes from different regions of Ecuador, their ingredients, and how they are prepared. I also want to show how food is an important part of Ecuadorian culture and traditions.

Scenarios

  • What are the most popular traditional dishes in Ecuador?
  • What ingredients are used to prepare traditional Ecuadorian food?
  • What traditional dishes can I find in the Coast, Highlands, and Amazon regions of Ecuador?

Color Schema

Dark Green (#123B2A): This is the primary color of the website. It will be used for the header, footer, and main headings.

Light Green (#5A9B78): This color will be used for secondary elements and highlights.

Dark Purple (#54217D): This color will be used for subheadings, borders, and decorative details.

Light Purple (#A477C7): This color will be used for accents and other decorative elements.

Typography

Roboto Bold will be used for headings and section titles.

Roboto Regular will be used for paragraphs, food descriptions, and navigation.
