Design Principles

Contrast

Contrast helps important elements stand out by using differences in color, size, shape, or typography.

Alignment

Alignment creates order by visually connecting elements and making the page easier to read.

Proximity

Proximity means placing related elements close together so users can easily understand that they belong to the same group.

Repetition

Repetition creates consistency by reusing colors, fonts, spacing, and design elements throughout a website.

White Space

White space gives elements room to breathe and improves readability and visual organization.
